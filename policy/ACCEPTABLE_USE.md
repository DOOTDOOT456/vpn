# Acceptable Use Policy (AUP)

_By using the hotspot or the tunnel, you agree to these rules._

## Prohibited on the tunnel

You may not use this service to:

1. **Break the law** — in the operator's jurisdiction, yours, or the
   destination site's. This includes copyright infringement, harassment,
   threats, and distributing malware.
2. **Attack other systems** — port scanning, brute-forcing, DoS/DDoS,
   exploiting, or unauthorized access to any system or account.
3. **Spam or bulk abuse** — mass mailing, scraping at abusive rates, or
   automated account creation.
4. **Impersonate others** or attempt to hide criminal activity behind the
   operator's IP.
5. **Circumvent the hotspot itself** — attempting to access the Pi, the
   broker, other hotspot users' devices, or the local network behind the
   ESP8266.

## Prohibited at the hotspot layer

- Sharing the hotspot password with non-members without the operator's
  permission.
- Attempting to reflash, reset, or physically tamper with the WeMos/ESP8266.
- Running your own router/DHCP server on the hotspot.

## Fair use

- The exit node shares ~0.3–1 Mbps among everyone. Heavy downloads and
  video streaming degrade the service for all users — keep bulk transfers
  off peak hours.
- Paid supporters get QoS priority per the tier table in the README; that is
  the only difference between tiers.

## Enforcement

The operator may, at their discretion and without notice:

- Disconnect any client from the hotspot,
- Rotate the preshared key (revoking all users until re-issued),
- Refuse service to anyone.

Serious violations (attacks originating from the exit node) may be reported
to the operator's ISP or law enforcement — the exit IP belongs to the
operator, and they are accountable for it.
